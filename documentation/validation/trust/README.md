`make sim-trust SCENARIO=baseline` wrote this report; do not edit it. Each run replays
the bans of the trace and the verdicts of publishers of known behavior
through the store, allow-list and decision engine of one observer node,
and scores the bans that the engine enforces against the ground truth.
[ADR 0034](../../adr/0034-trust-simulation-by-trace-replay.md) defines the world, the models and every metric.

A value is the mean over the seeds ± half the width of its 95 % confidence
interval (Student t). A dash means the metric is not defined, e.g. precision
without bans; (n=k) marks a value defined in only k seeds.

## Findings

## Files

A mean and the bounds of its interval are rounded to the second
significant digit of the interval's half-width, beyond which the seeds do
not resolve them; every value has at most 6 significant digits.

- `summary.csv`: every metric of every configuration at the end, cumulatively,
  and the false bans by the class of their victim (`false_bans_cdn`, `false_bans_crawler`, `false_bans_customer`, `false_bans_nat`):
  `model,fraction,profile,metric,n,mean,ci_low,ci_high`.
- `hourly.csv.gz`: every metric of every configuration in every hour,
  cumulatively and within the hour, except the durations per key, which
  have a value only cumulatively (`neutralized_share`, `neutralization_hours`, `neutralization_events`, `newcomers_converged`, `newcomer_convergence_hours`, `whitewash_payoff`, `keys_burned`):
  `model,fraction,profile,hour,window,metric,n,mean,ci_low,ci_high`.
- `feeds.csv`: the feed metrics of every role: `model,fraction,profile,role,metric,n,mean,ci_low,ci_high`.
- `publishers.csv.gz`: the feed metrics of every publisher key of every run:
  `model,fraction,profile,seed,slot,key,role,volume,exclusive,latency_minutes,bound,accuracy`.
- `corroboration.csv`: the attackers by the most trusted honest remotes at
  once and by whether the observer reported them, and the share banned.

## Limitations

- The trace is synthetic unless the header names a recorded one. Its
  parameters are in ADR 0034; the licensing of real operators' Fail2Ban
  logs and their treatment under the DSGVO are not settled yet.
- One observer trusts every publisher with the same weight. Verdicts
  reach it the second they are issued; routing is measured apart.
- The store is swept every 5 virtual minutes, so a ban whose score
  falls when one of its verdicts expires may last up to 5 minutes longer.
- An address is an attacker or benign for the whole run; addresses that
  change hands are not modeled.
